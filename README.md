# 📊 Sales Dashboard – Power BI

An interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze sales performance, profitability, customers, products, regions, teams, and business segments.

The dashboard provides multiple analytical views that help identify sales trends, profitable products, regional performance, customer performance, and changes in revenue and profit over time.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on transforming sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard using Power BI.

The dashboard contains multiple pages covering:

* Sales Performance
* Sales Trends
* Customer & Product Summary
* Profit & Revenue Analysis
* Segment Analysis
* Profit Analysis

Interactive filters allow users to analyze the data based on:

* Date
* Region
* Segment
* Team
* Period

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

* Analyze overall sales performance
* Monitor total sales, quantity, orders, and customers
* Track sales trends over time
* Identify top-performing and under-performing products
* Analyze customer sales performance
* Compare regional sales and profitability
* Analyze business segments and teams
* Evaluate profit margins
* Understand revenue and profit contribution
* Provide an interactive dashboard for business decision-making

---

## 📊 Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Performance Dashboard

Provides an overall summary of business performance.

**Key KPIs:**

* Total Sales
* Total Quantity
* Total Orders
* Total Customers
* Profit Margin %
* Total Profit

**Visualizations include:**

* Total Sales by Date
* Total Sales by Category
* Total Quantity and Orders by Date
* Total Sales by Region

---

### 2. Sales Trend

Analyzes how sales performance changes over time.

**Visualizations include:**

* Total Sales by Month
* Total Orders by Month
* Profit Margin % by Month
* Sales Growth % by Month

The page also supports filtering by:

* Period
* Region
* Segment
* Team
* Date


---

### 3. Customer & Product Summary

Provides a detailed view of customer and product performance.

**Analysis includes:**

* Total Sales by Customer
* Total Sales by Product
* Most Selling Product
* Least Selling Product

This page can help identify high-value customers and products contributing significantly to sales.


---

### 4. Profit & Revenue Analysis

Focuses on revenue and profitability trends.

**Key KPIs:**

* Total Sales
* Profit
* Profit Margin %
* Average Revenue per ...

**Visualizations include:**

* Total Sales by Start of Month
* Total Orders by Start of Month
* Cumulative Sales by Month
* Cumulative Profit by Month


---

### 5. Segment Analysis

Compares sales and profit across different business dimensions.

**Analysis includes:**

* Sales & Profit by Category
* Sales & Profit by Segment
* Sales & Profit by Region
* Sales & Profit by Team

This provides a comparative view of business performance across different categories and organizational segments.

---

### 6. Profit Analysis

Provides a detailed analysis of product profitability.

**Visualizations include:**

* Profit Margin % by Product
* Contribution Margin by Product
* Total Sales & Total Variable Cost by Product
* Total Sales, Variable Cost & Profit by Product

This page helps identify products with stronger margins and understand their contribution to overall profitability.


---

## 🔑 Key Metrics

The dashboard tracks several important business KPIs:

| KPI                 | Description                                   |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Total Sales         | Overall revenue generated                     |
| Total Quantity      | Total number of products sold                 |
| Total Orders        | Number of orders                              |
| Total Customers     | Number of customers                           |
| Profit              | Overall profit generated                      |
| Profit Margin %     | Profit relative to sales                      |
| Sales Growth %      | Change in sales over time                     |
| Contribution Margin | Contribution of products toward profitability |

## 🚀 How to Use

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. Install **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Open `Sales_Dashboard.pbix`.
4. Interact with the dashboard using the available filters and slicers.
5. Explore the different dashboard pages.
